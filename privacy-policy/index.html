GOOGLE OAUTH · DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Huy Hanh Marketing and the n8n OAuth application access, use, store, protect, and delete information, including data obtained through Google APIs.

Effective: August 27, 2026Application: n8n OAuthDomain: huyhanhmarketing.io.vn

1. Who we are

Huy Hanh Marketing operates digital marketing and automation services at huyhanhmarketing.io.vn. This policy also applies to the Google OAuth application identified on the consent screen as n8n OAuth, used to connect authorized Google services with user-configured automation workflows.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the Google services and permissions that you explicitly authorize, the application may process:

  • Basic Google account information such as name, email address, and account identifier.
  • OAuth authorization credentials and tokens required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Google service data only within the OAuth scopes displayed to you and approved by you.
  • Workflow inputs, outputs, timestamps, technical logs, and error information needed to operate, secure, or troubleshoot the service.

The exact permissions requested are shown by Google before access is granted. We do not access Google user data outside the permissions you approve.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing functionality requested by the authorized user. This can include retrieving or processing information needed by an automation, creating or updating information through a Google service when explicitly configured, generating reports or alerts, maintaining the authorized connection, and diagnosing failures.

Limited Use commitment: Google user data is not sold, rented, provided to data brokers, or used for targeted advertising. Google Workspace API data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell Google user data. Information may be transferred only when necessary for functionality requested by the user, including to infrastructure or service providers acting on our behalf, to a third-party service or recipient explicitly selected by the user in a workflow, or when disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect the security of the service.

5. Data storage and security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. OAuth credentials and access tokens are treated as confidential authentication information and are used only to maintain authorized service connections.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate authorized workflows, maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Users may request deletion of personal information controlled by Huy Hanh Marketing by contacting huyhanh.hum@gmail.com or admin@huyhanhmarketing.io.vn.

When Google access is revoked or an integration is discontinued, the application will no longer use that authorization to access Google services. Limited operational logs may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, debugging, or legal compliance.

7. Your choices and control

You may decline requested OAuth permissions, disable a workflow, disconnect an integration, revoke the application's Google Account access through your Google Account security settings, or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information that we control, subject to applicable law.

8. Third-party services

Automation workflows may interact with third-party services selected by the user, including Google, n8n, messaging platforms, advertising platforms, CRM systems, and hosting providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children under the age required to independently consent to online services in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the OAuth application.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, requested Google API scopes, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material revisions are published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or data deletion requests, contact huyhanh.hum@gmail.com or admin@huyhanhmarketing.io.vn.
