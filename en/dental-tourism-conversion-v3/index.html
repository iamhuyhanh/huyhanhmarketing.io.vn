Common questions

What international patients usually want to know first

How many days should I stay in Vietnam?

It depends on the treatment, healing requirements and whether multiple visits are needed. Your recommended stay should be discussed after the initial clinical review.

Can I get an exact quote before flying?

An initial estimate can be prepared from available records. The final cost may require confirmation after clinical examination and imaging.

Can I speak with the clinic before booking my trip?

Yes. International enquiries can be discussed remotely before travel so you can understand likely options and next steps.

What happens after I return home?

The clinic can provide post-treatment instructions and remote follow-up support where clinically appropriate. Your specific follow-up schedule depends on the treatment performed.
