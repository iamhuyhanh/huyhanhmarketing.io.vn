Publishing flow

How it works

1
Connect

Sign in with TikTok and authorize the requested publishing scope.

2
Review account

We retrieve creator information and the privacy options available for that account.

3
Review content

Check the video, caption and interaction settings before sending.

4
Confirm publish

The post is submitted only after the creator confirms the action.

Educational use: content produced through this service is for general dental education and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a licensed clinician.

Account control and transparency

Users can revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings. Access tokens are used only to provide the features the user has authorized. We do not sell personal information.
